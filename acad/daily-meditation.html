A simple TToB evening review

Facts before verdicts. Notice one thing that is true. Notice one feeling without prosecuting it. Name three things you did well. If a repair is needed, name one. Then choose one kind, dignified next action.

Ask: Am I here to learn from today - or convict myself for it?

The Strengthening My Recovery button opens the official Adult Children daily-meditation resource. The evening-review prompt is TToB material and is not official ACAD literature.
