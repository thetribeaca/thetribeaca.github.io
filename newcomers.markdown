## Welcome to The Tribe of Brothers (TToB).

We are a **Men's Movement** who work the Adult Children of Alcoholics and Dysfunctional Families (ACAD) programme.

**Our primary purpose is to recover from the toxic effects of growing up in addictted and dysfunctional family, and to help the millions of men out there who are still struggling & suffering from these symptoms, and denial.**

This is an insidious disease. It takes many forms. If we said 80% of the planet suffers from this? You might laugh at us. 

If you identify with Workaholism, Alcoholism, Dysfunctional Relationships (They Just Keep Going Wrong?), Love & Sex Addictions, Game & Gambling Addictions, Shame/Anger/Rage Addictions or just Powerful Feelings that seem to get you in Trouble...often.
