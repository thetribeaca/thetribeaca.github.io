## Welcome to The Tribe.

We are a **men's group** who work the Adult Children of Alcoholics and Dysfunctional Families (ACAD) programme.

**Our primary purpose is to recover from the toxic effects of growing up in addictive and dysfunctional families, and to help the millions of men out there who are still suffering.**
