## Welcome to The Tribe of Brothers (TToB).

We are a **Men's Movement** who work the Adult Children of Alcoholics and Dysfunctional Families (ACAD) programme.

**Our primary purpose is to recover from the toxic effects of growing up in addictted and dysfunctional families, and to help the millions of men out there who are still struggling & suffering.**
